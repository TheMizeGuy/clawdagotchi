## What it is

- An orange pixel-art Claude (the Claude Code mascot) drawn as the terminal's `Image` element, 8 cells by 2 rows (0.3.0; 4 by 1 with the `big` option off), through the `SessionMode` render site. Since 0.4.0 the frames are named (`plugins/mize-coworker/scripts/frames.json`, drawn by `scripts/make-frames.py` at 4x as hard pixels); 0.5.0 has 73 (0.6.0: 132): 35 solo frames (0.6.0: 94) and 38 scenes of 12x2 cells (Claude and a prop). The code names frames by name (`hooks/lib/sprite.ts` mirrors the JSON; `tests/test_frames.py` holds the two equal); each names one of the twelve 0.1.0 braille poses, which stays the picture's `alt` and the `picture: false` drawing in the same orange `#d97757`.
- While the main loop's turn runs, Claude sits beside the spinner, left of the engine's whole line, and the footer keeps only its labels; when the turn ends he is back on the status row (0.2.0, option `besideSpinner`). Since 0.4.0 he sits there in a scene for the activity: a thought bubble filling with dots, a book, a magnifying glass over lines of text, a laptop, a terminal printing output, a globe, one to three helpers for the delegating calls in flight, a speech bubble, a gear (option `scenes`). Since 0.5.0 every spinner word has its own: `sceneFor(frame, word, mode)` swaps the scene beside the spinner, step for step, for a pen on a notepad while `Writing` (mode `responding`), the laptop while the model writes a tool call's input (`tool-input`), the gear while `Working` (`tool-use`), the magnifying glass over the globe for `Searching the web`, a scroll for `Loading a skill` and a plug for `Calling <server>`. The footer draws each scene's solo frame and never swaps.
- Idle on the status row he breathes (idle and idleUp every 2 s) and blinks every 6 s (0.5.0: half shut 100 ms, shut 200 ms, half shut 100 ms); every 12 to 30 s he strolls to another spot (one cell per 250 ms tick), looks around, hops, yawns or sips a coffee, inside the cells the mod reserves; a seeded generator, no `Math.random` (0.2.0; 0.4.0 the breath, the yawn and the sip, part of `gestures`). Three moves need a reason: a sweat at 80% of context or more, an hourglass while a usage window is `over` or `crit`, a pumpkin from 24 to 31 October (option `seasonal`); when one applies it is about one move in three.
- While the main loop rests and agents or workflows work in the background he minds them (0.6.0, activity `supervising`): at ease as when idle, the caption `N agents`, and a skit every 4 to 10 s from a mix of fifteen (a rocket as they start, a helper with a finished agent's report, a paddle with their count, a radar, a headset, a baton while a workflow runs, a helper on his head, juggling, bubble gum, popcorn, a paper plane, meditation, a plant that grows with the wait, a lantern at night, a sandwich at noon), never the same twice running and never the hop. A background shell keeps nothing busy: he idles, with a glance at a small terminal among his moves. 59 new solo frames: 132 in all, 94 solo and 38 scenes.
- A turn that ends with an answer ends in a hop; one of two minutes or more in a cheer with confetti (0.4.0). Asleep after 10 idle minutes he breathes in a slow loop (a frame every 3 s), frosted once the prompt cache has gone cold (0.4.0).
- The session's vitals reach him through the status bus the other way round (0.4.0): the status line script writes `<session>/.vitals` (context percent, the 5h and 7d window states, the cache state; `shared/statusline-bus.ts` `parseVitals`), and the mod reads it at most once every 10 s, only when an idle Claude picks a move or a sleeping one turns over.
- The main loop's spinner word becomes what is happening (`Reading register.ts`, `Running npm`). Only the program name of a Bash command is ever shown, never an argument.
- `/coworker` reports it; `/coworker off` and `/coworker on`, typed at the prompt, switch it for the session. `/coworker demo` (0.5.0), typed at the prompt, plays every animation once in the band above the prompt (`AbovePrompt`), 30 acts in 65.5 s (0.6.0: 46 acts in 120.75 s, the skits included), each the picture with a dim label beside its lower row holding the spinner word or move name and the frame (`Writing · write2`); it writes only its own `$.state` value, and a second `/coworker demo`, `/coworker off` or a main turn starting ends it. Options `sprite`, `picture`, `big`, `besideSpinner`, `scenes`, `narrate`, `animate`, `gestures`, `seasonal`.
- 0.3.0 reserves 22 cells on the status row (11 below 110 columns) for the 8-cell picture; with `big` or `picture` off, 18 and 9. Since 0.5.0 the mod also keeps a machine-wide default of that reserve, `~/.claude/state/statusline/bus/.reserve-default` (`shared/statusline-bus.ts`: `RESERVE_DEFAULT_FILE`, `reserveDefaultPath`), which the status line holds for a session with no `.reserve` of its own yet: written at session start and when the session moves, when it differs or is a day old, never by a release; and a session that reserves nothing writes its own empty `.reserve` so the default does not apply to it.

## What the live sessions settled

Driven in a pseudo-terminal on 2.1.287 (`claude --plugin-dir`, the screen read back through a terminal emulator by `scripts/tui-capture.py`):

- The `SessionMode` site is not a footer row of its own: the engine draws it right-aligned on the SAME row as the status line (`~/.claude/statusline.sh`), and wraps it onto an extra row when the two do not fit. A caption coming and going would then move the prompt, so the mod reserves its cells on the status bus (`shared/statusline-bus.ts`, the `.reserve` file) and the script keeps them free; below 110 columns the mod draws Claude without the caption.
- The site is first rendered before `session.start` has run. A hook that passes then never reads `$.state`, so later writes do not redraw it: the mod asks for a redraw (`$.ui.invalidate('ui.render')`) once the session is known to be interactive.
- The main loop's spinner is raised under the session's id on the terminal, not under `main`.
- The `Image` element works in the `SessionMode` site: in Ghostty 1.3.1 (kitty graphics) a 68x40 RGBA picture in `columns={4} rows={1}` drew as crisp pixels on the status row (checked by hand, 2026-10-02). A terminal without the protocol, and the pty harness, draw the `alt` text dim in its place, and size the box to the `alt` (a 5-cell `alt` took 5 cells in a 4-column box), so the mod counts Claude as 5 cells in its reserve sums.
- In a `ui.render` hook, `await next(e)` resolves to `{ "type": "engine", "ref": 1 }`: a reference to the engine's own drawing that can be placed as a child of the hook's own tree. A Spinner hook returning a row of the picture, a space and that reference draws Claude left of the engine's full spinner line, elapsed time and tokens included.
- The engine's spinner drawing opens with a blank row above its line: placed at the row's top, Claude sat on that blank row with the line one row below (0.2.0's first live run). The one-row picture now steps one row down (`marginTop={1}`) and sits on the line: `⢼⢽⠿⡯⡧ ✳ Writing…` in the pty capture.

Checked by hand in a real Ghostty 1.3.1 window on 2.1.288 (0.3.0):

- An `Image` of `columns={8} rows={2}` in the `SessionMode` site draws right-aligned and spans the status line row and the row under it, the engine's permission-mode row, which is short and leaves that space empty. No row is added to the footer; the status line and the mode row keep their places. A `Text` beside the picture sits on the first (status) row, so the caption, the ` z` and the wander's blank cells stay there.
- In the `Spinner` site, a row of `<Box flexDirection="row" flexShrink={0}>` holding the 8x2 `Image` and a space, then `await next(e)`, draws Claude spanning the engine's blank row and the spinner line, the line's text starting on the second row right of him. The two-row picture needs no `marginTop` step-down.

## What the 0.6.0 work settled (2026-10-03)

- The stuck hop, reproduced in a pseudo-terminal at 100 columns on 2.1.288: a session told to run `sleep 240` in a background shell and stop showed the hop pose in six of twelve snapshots taken half a second apart, a minute after its turn ended. The stop hook's `background_tasks` held one `shell` task, the model showed `delegating` for any task there, and the footer drew that loop's solo frames, hop2 and idle. With 0.6.0 the same session showed idle, four blinks and one step of a stroll in 41 snapshots over 20 s.
- `classic.Stop`'s `background_tasks` names each task's `type` (`shell`, `subagent`, `monitor`, `workflow`): read by type alone, a shell or a monitor is not agent work. With a background Agent call the engine also draws a list of the agents under the permission-mode row (`⏺ main`, `◯ general-purpose ...`); the status row and the mode row keep their places above it, and the two-row picture still spans them.
- A render hook may not write `$.state`: `state.set: denied: it was made while ui.render is being dispatched, and drawing is pure`. The spinner's hook therefore only notes the time it drew him; what follows from the spinner being back (the main loop is at work again) is worked out by the next breath or the next step of a move, which are timer events.
- `classic.PreToolUse` carries no agent id, so the model cannot tell an agent's call from the main loop's while the main turn runs. Between main turns it can: the main loop calls nothing then, so a call that starts there is some agent's.
- In a live session with one background agent (`claude --plugin-dir`, sonnet, 100 columns), `/coworker` read `Claude is minding 1 agent in the background (busy for 43s)`; over 75 s of snapshots the status row showed the skits' poses between stretches of idle, one hop (the notification turn ending with an answer) and idle once the agent was done. The debug log held no failed or skipped hook of this mod.
- An `Image` that changes width under one key keeps its earlier placement. In Ghostty 1.3.1 at 120 columns every solo frame of the tour was drawn two cells right of its box and cut at the box's right edge: after a 12-cell scene, an 8-cell picture under the same key (`demo`) was placed as if centered in the scene's box, (12 - 8) / 2 cells in. The installed 0.5.4 did the same (the wave's arm, the mug, the hourglass missing in the band), so the fixes of 0.5.2 to 0.5.4 (padding by spaces, a box around the picture) had not reached it. Keying the picture by its width (`demo-${columns}`, and `claude-${columns}` beside the spinner, where a flinch comes between scenes) makes each width its own element; the same captures then showed every solo frame whole, in the band and beside the spinner. The footer's picture never changes width in a session and keeps its key.
- Seeing the pictures needs a real Ghostty window (the pty harness shows the `alt`): the Ghostty binary launched with `--command=<script>`, the script a small pty proxy that runs `claude --plugin-dir <worktree copy>` in a nested pty, passes everything through both ways and types the scripted keys, touching a marker file at each moment to capture; a watcher outside finds the window by its owner pid (CGWindowList) and runs `screencapture -l <id>`. `--window-width=100` gave a window of 120 columns here: read the width off the screen, not off the flag.
- The art was drawn by four agents in parallel, each in a private copy of `scripts/` with marked blocks to fill, then a critic and a reviser per group; the merge takes the marked blocks and the manifest entries and refuses a sandbox that changed anything outside them. The existing 73 PNGs stayed byte-identical.

## Safety

All mods share one worker, so every hook and timer body catches what it calls. The mod runs no process, makes no model or network call, changes no tool call and adds nothing to the model's context. Its files are the reserve and the machine-wide default of it; the ones it reads are that default (at session start and when the session moves) and the vitals, at most once in 10 s and never while busy. Of a background task it reads the type and the status, never the command or the description. A headless session registers the command and does nothing else.
