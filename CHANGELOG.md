## mize-coworker 0.5.0 (2026-10-02)

- Every spinner word has its own scene beside the spinner. The owner asked to "ensure claud has animations for all his different spinner words like working thinking etc all of them and REALLY make this look SUPER polished". `sceneFor(frame, word, mode)` in `hooks/lib/sprite.ts` swaps the scene the Spinner hook draws, step for step: the thought bubble becomes a pen on a notepad while `Writing` (mode `responding`), the laptop while the model writes a tool call's input (`tool-input`) and the gear while `Working` (`tool-use`: work1, work2, work1); the globe takes the magnifying glass for `Searching the web`; the gear becomes a scroll for `Loading a skill` and a plug for `Calling <server>`. Every other frame, word and mode is unchanged, the state keeps the loop's own frame, and the footer never swaps. The doing is written whenever Claude is drawn, so with `narrate` off the scene still follows what is happening.
- 73 frames (from 62): `blinkHalf` and the scenes write1-3, webSearch1-3, skill1-2, plug1-2 join the contract (`scripts/frames.json`) and the table in `sprite.ts`. `tests/test_frames.py` holds the table equal to the JSON kind by kind, since the JSON now lists a solo frame after the scenes.
- Softer timings: the idle blink closes and opens (blinkHalf 100 ms, blink 200 ms, blinkHalf 100 ms, still every 6 s); the greeting's wave holds each side two ticks; the yawn and the sip ease in and out around a held middle (two, four, two ticks); the pumpkin's glow flickers unevenly (three, two, three, two). Every busy loop, swapped scene and move keeps its longest hold under the spinner's 2.5 s grace less 500 ms, as a test holds.
- `/coworker demo`, typed at the prompt: a tour of every animation in the band above the prompt (`AbovePrompt`), 30 acts in 65.5 s, each a row of the picture (a scene 12x2, a solo frame 8x2, in a seat 13 cells wide) and a dim label with the spinner word or move name and the frame (`Writing · write2`): every busy scene family as the spinner shows it (the swapped ones, and the team of one, two and three helpers), the greeting, the hop, the cheer, the flinch, the breath and the blink, every idle move, and the warm and cold sleep sped up. It ends by itself, at `/coworker demo` again, `/coworker off`, a main turn starting, a session move or its end; it writes one `$.state` value (`demo`) and runs one `$.clock.after` at a time, and never the footer's view or spot, the doing or the reserve. It draws over whatever the other mods draw in the band. `/coworker` mentions it, and the act on screen while it plays.
- The default reserve: `shared/statusline-bus.ts` gains `RESERVE_DEFAULT_FILE` (`.reserve-default`) and `reserveDefaultPath`, documented beside `.reserve`, and is vendored again into the plugin (`hooks/lib/statusline-bus.ts`). At session start, and when a `/clear`, `/resume` or `/branch` moves the session, the mod writes its reserve line to `~/.claude/state/statusline/bus/.reserve-default` when the file is missing, says something else or is a day old (the status line's sweep deletes bus files untouched for three days); a release never writes it. The status line holds the default's cells for a session with no `.reserve` of its own yet, so the footer no longer wraps onto its own row for a refresh at session start (0.4.0). A session that reserves nothing (the `sprite` option off, `off` adopted on a reload) now writes its own empty `.reserve`, so the default does not hold cells for it.
- README: the reserve numbers are `22 11 110` and `18 9 110` (it said `22 10 110` and `18 8 110`), and the footer takes Claude back after the 2.5 s grace (it said one second).
- 158 tests (from 123), plus the 6 of `tests/test_frames.py`: the swap for every word and mode, the footer unchanged, the blink on a mock clock, the timings, the tour step by step through the engine, its band and every way it stops, the default reserve, and the crash-safety suite now witnessing the band and the tour's timer against a refusing host. Checked live in a pseudo-terminal at 120 columns on 2.1.288.

## mize-coworker 0.4.0 (2026-10-02)

- Claude has 62 named frames (was 12): 34 solo frames and 28 scenes of 12x2 cells, Claude and a prop. The owner asked for "a very nice really quality giga high quality visual pass and upgrade to the entire status bar and claud and his anmations get creative". The frame contract is `scripts/frames.json`; `hooks/lib/sprite.ts` mirrors it and the code names frames by name: the `$.state` view carries the frame's name beside its braille, and nothing looks a frame up by its braille any more (several frames share one of the twelve braille poses, which stay the `alt` and the `picture: false` drawing). `tests/test_frames.py` holds the table equal to the JSON and checks every PNG's size, outside the engine, because `claude plugin test` has no file system.
- Beside the spinner, each busy activity is a scene: a thought bubble filling with dots, a book with turning pages, a magnifying glass sweeping lines of text, a laptop, a terminal printing output, a turning globe, one to three small helpers (one per delegating call in flight), a speech bubble when he needs you, a gear. His seat there keeps a scene's width, so the spinner line does not jump when a solo frame (the flinch) comes between two scenes. The footer always draws the scene's solo frame, so the reserve is unchanged (`22 10 110`). New option `scenes` (default on); off, solo frames beside the spinner too.
- Done: a hop played once (crouch, air, landing) after a turn under two minutes; a cheer with confetti after a longer one, by `turn.complete`'s `durationMs`, else the turn start the mod saw.
- Idle life: he breathes (idle and idleUp every 2 s, one slow timer), still blinks every 6 s, and his moves gain a three-frame hop, a yawn and a coffee sip (yawns grow more common after 6 idle minutes, as his nap nears). Three moves need a reason: a sweat when the context window is 80% full or more, an hourglass while the 5h or 7d usage window is `over` or `crit`, a pumpkin from 24 to 31 October local time (new option `seasonal`, default on); when one applies it is about one move in three. Asleep, a slow loop (a frame every 3 s, one timer), frosted once the prompt cache has gone cold.
- The status bus runs the other way too: `shared/statusline-bus.ts` gains `VITALS_FILE`, `vitalsPath` and a total `parseVitals` for `<session>/.vitals` (`<context>\t<5h>\t<7d>\t<cache>`), which the status line script writes; vendored into the plugin. The mod reads it at most once every 10 s, only when an idle Claude picks a move or a sleeping one turns over; a missing file is unknown.
- 123 tests (from 92), plus the 5 of `tests/test_frames.py`; the crash-safety suite covers the breath, the sleep loop and the vitals read against a refusing host and a vitals file of anything at all. Checked live in a pseudo-terminal at 100 columns on 2.1.288.

## mize-coworker 0.2.0 (2026-10-02)

- Claude is orange pixel art now: twelve 100x60 PNG frames (`assets/frames/`, drawn by `scripts/make-frames.py`) shown with the terminal's `Image` element in 4x1 cells, where the terminal speaks kitty graphics (Ghostty). - `$.state` gains `spot` (`where` and `x`), written only when it changes. 87 tests (from 71); checked live in a pseudo-terminal: the `alt` on the status row idle and after the turn, and on the spinner's row during it.

## mize-coworker 0.1.0 (2026-10-02)

- New mod: Claude as a small coworker on the status row. A 5-cell braille Claude (the Claude Code mascot) in the theme's Claude color sits at the right end of the status line row (the `SessionMode` render site) and animates with what the session and its agents are doing: thinking, reading, searching, editing, running, browsing, delegating, needing the owner, idle, asleep. The main loop's spinner word says what is happening (`Reading register.ts`, `Running npm`; only a command's program name, never an argument). `/coworker`, `/coworker off`, `/coworker on`; options `sprite`, `narrate`, `animate`.
- The mod reserves its cells on the status row through the status bus (`<session>/.reserve`), so the status line script keeps them free and the engine never wraps the site onto a row of its own; below 110 columns it draws Claude without the caption.
- It only draws: no process, no model or network call, no tool call changed, nothing in the model's context; every hook and timer body catches what it calls. Verified in live interactive sessions driven in a pseudo-terminal at four widths.
