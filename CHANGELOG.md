## mize-coworker 0.6.0 (2026-10-03)

- The stuck hop is gone. The owner: "fix the claud guy where he gets stuck just jumping repeatedly in the bottom right corner". Any background task the main loop's stop reported, a plain `sleep` in a background shell included, showed as `delegating`, and on the status row that loop's solo frames were hop2 and idle in turn, a hop every half second for as long as the task ran and for up to 30 minutes after it was last heard of (reproduced in a pseudo-terminal: one background shell, six of twelve snapshots half a second apart in the hop pose). Now a stop report is read by task type (`backgroundOf`): shells and monitors keep nothing busy (he idles and sleeps as ever, with a `peek` at a small terminal among his idle moves); agents and workflows are agent work, which he minds (below). And the hop is never looped without the helpers, whatever happens: wherever the team scene itself cannot be drawn (the status row, or beside the spinner with `scenes` or `big` off) its stand-in is a look toward where they would be (`footerOf`); the hop stays in the scene, where the helpers bob with him.
- Minding agents (`supervising`), the owner's "add some fun creative stuff when the main session is idle and agents are working or workflows". While the main loop rests (no turn, or a turn left open with no spinner drawn, as between a `/goal`'s iterations) and agents or workflows are at work in the background, he stands at ease as when idle (the breath, the blink, the caption `N agents` where there is room) and plays a skit every 4 to 10 seconds, never the same twice running and never the hop: `launch` (a rocket lifts off as the agents take over from the main turn), `report` (a helper brings a page when an agent finishes, at most one in 20 s), `tally` (a judge's paddle with how many are at work, 1 to 9 and `9+`), `radar`, `radio` (a headset: mission control), `conduct` (a baton and notes, while a workflow runs), `perch` (a helper on his head), `juggle`, `gum`, `popcorn`, `plane` (a paper plane that comes back), `zen`, `garden` (a potted plant he waters, which grows with the wait: leaves after 2 minutes, a bud after 6, a flower after 15), `lantern` (22:00 to 06:00) and `lunch` (12:00 to 13:00), the last two under the `seasonal` option. The everyday looks, strolls, sips and yawns are in the mix. `gestures` off leaves the breath and the blink.
- The model behind it (`hooks/lib/activity.ts`): agents are at work while a call of one is in flight, or one was heard from or a main stop reported some within the last 10 minutes; an agent's `turn.complete` and a main stop that reports none take that back at once, and an agent's own stop can only lower the count. `classic.PreToolUse` carries no agent id, so a call that starts with no main turn running is taken for some agent's, and one still in flight when the main loop stops with agents reported becomes one of theirs. Only an agent that ended with an answer brings a report. An independent review (three reviewers, two verifiers) found 19 things after the first pass, two of them wrong behavior (a live agent's long call forgotten at the main turn's end; strolls walking in place beside the caption); all but one are fixed here, and that one (a spinner showing the engine's own message counts as the main loop resting) is in the README's limits. Ten quiet minutes end in sleep, and a sign of an agent wakes him to it again. `/coworker` says `minding N agents in the background`.
- 132 frames (from 73): 59 new solo frames, drawn by four parallel artists in sandboxes, each reviewed by a critic and revised, then merged into `scripts/make-frames.py` in four marked blocks. `frames.json`, the table in `sprite.ts` and `tests/test_frames.py` hold them.
- `/coworker demo` shows them: 46 acts in 120.75 s (from 30 in 65.5 s), the skits each once, the tally for three agents and the plant through its four stages.
- The wake timer no longer moves for a change of less than a minute later (every agent event moved the end of the quiet), and a render hook writes no state: the spinner's hook only notes when it drew him, and the breath or a move's next step sees that the main loop is back.
- `/coworker demo` and the seat beside the spinner draw solo frames whole again. Under one key, an `Image` that changed width kept its earlier placement: every solo frame after a scene was drawn two cells right and cut at its box's edge (the wave's arm, the mug, the hourglass, and every new skit's prop gone; seen in Ghostty at 120 columns on the installed 0.5.4 too, whose 0.5.2 to 0.5.4 entries took it for a centering in the row). The pictures there are now keyed by their width (`demo-8`, `demo-12`, `claude-8`, `claude-12`), which makes each width its own element.
- 190 tests (from 159), plus the 6 of `tests/test_frames.py`. Checked live on 2.1.288. In a pseudo-terminal at 100 columns: with a background shell 41 snapshots over 20 s showed idle, four blinks and one stroll step and no hop; with a background agent `/coworker` read `Claude is minding 1 agent in the background (busy for 43s)`, the status row went through the skits' poses, and he was idle again once the agent's result came in; the debug logs held no failed or skipped hook of this mod. In real Ghostty 1.3.1 windows (a pty proxy typing the script, `screencapture` of the window): the turn's end with a background agent drew the hop, then `1 agent`, then the rocket on its pad, lit and gone; over the next minutes the paper plane, the juggling, the meditation, the headset, the bubble gum and the watering can drew whole on the status row; the tour drew every skit whole in the band; and a failing call's flinch drew whole beside the spinner between two scenes.

## mize-coworker 0.5.0 (2026-10-02)

- Every spinner word has its own scene beside the spinner. The owner asked to "ensure claud has animations for all his different spinner words like working thinking etc all of them and REALLY make this look SUPER polished". `sceneFor(frame, word, mode)` in `hooks/lib/sprite.ts` swaps the scene the Spinner hook draws, step for step: the thought bubble becomes a pen on a notepad while `Writing` (mode `responding`), the laptop while the model writes a tool call's input (`tool-input`) and the gear while `Working` (`tool-use`: work1, work2, work1); the globe takes the magnifying glass for `Searching the web`; the gear becomes a scroll for `Loading a skill` and a plug for `Calling <server>`. Every other frame, word and mode is unchanged, the state keeps the loop's own frame, and the footer never swaps. The doing is written whenever Claude is drawn, so with `narrate` off the scene still follows what is happening.
- 73 frames (from 62): `blinkHalf` and the scenes write1-3, webSearch1-3, skill1-2, plug1-2 join the contract (`scripts/frames.json`) and the table in `sprite.ts`. `tests/test_frames.py` holds the table equal to the JSON kind by kind, since the JSON now lists a solo frame after the scenes.
- Softer timings: the idle blink closes and opens (blinkHalf 100 ms, blink 200 ms, blinkHalf 100 ms, still every 6 s); the greeting's wave holds each side two ticks; the yawn and the sip ease in and out around a held middle (two, four, two ticks); the pumpkin's glow flickers unevenly (three, two, three, two). Every busy loop, swapped scene and move keeps its longest hold under the spinner's 2.5 s grace less 500 ms, as a test holds.
- `/coworker demo`, typed at the prompt: a tour of every animation in the band above the prompt (`AbovePrompt`), 30 acts in 65.5 s, each a row of the picture (a scene 12x2, a solo frame 8x2, in a seat 13 cells wide) and a dim label with the spinner word or move name and the frame (`Writing · write2`): every busy scene family as the spinner shows it (the swapped ones, and the team of one, two and three helpers), the greeting, the hop, the cheer, the flinch, the breath and the blink, every idle move, and the warm and cold sleep sped up. It ends by itself, at `/coworker demo` again, `/coworker off`, a main turn starting, a session move or its end; it writes one `$.state` value (`demo`) and runs one `$.clock.after` at a time, and never the footer's view or spot, the doing or the reserve. It draws over whatever the other mods draw in the band. `/coworker` mentions it, and the act on screen while it plays.
- The default reserve: `shared/statusline-bus.ts` gains `RESERVE_DEFAULT_FILE` (`.reserve-default`) and `reserveDefaultPath`, documented beside `.reserve`, and is vendored again into the plugin (`hooks/lib/statusline-bus.ts`). At session start, and when a `/clear`, `/resume` or `/branch` moves the session, the mod writes its reserve line to `~/.claude/state/statusline/bus/.reserve-default` when the file is missing, says something else or is a day old (the status line's sweep deletes bus files untouched for three days); a release never writes it. The status line holds the default's cells for a session with no `.reserve` of its own yet, so the footer no longer wraps onto its own row for a refresh at session start (0.4.0). A session that reserves nothing (the `sprite` option off, `off` adopted on a reload) now writes its own empty `.reserve`, so the default does not hold cells for it.
- README: the reserve numbers are `22 11 110` and `18 9 110` (it said `22 10 110` and `18 8 110`), and the footer takes Claude back after the 2.5 s grace (it said one second).
- 158 tests (from 123), plus the 6 of `tests/test_frames.py`: the swap for every word and mode, the footer unchanged, the blink on a mock clock, the timings, the tour step by step through the engine, its band and every way it stops, the default reserve, and the crash-safety suite now witnessing the band and the tour's timer against a refusing host.
